Born to be Epic

Founded by global adventurer Charlie Shepherd in Morocco in 2005, and co-owned by international hotelier and action woman, Carla Petzold-Back, Epic exists to deliver premium on-the-ground travel experiences in countries where our team lives and works.

We consider ourselves a rare breed, possessing not only the skills to create exciting travel designs but also solid, on-the-ground operational expertise. We ensure that expectations are not just met but brilliantly exceeded.

Charlie has 25 years of experience in the travel industry across three continents. His passion and expertise have been the driving force behind Epic for 15 years.
